Aether Rush: privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Aether Rush: Worlds Unbound ("the game") is made by Red Motion Studios. If you have a question about this policy, write to mayankkarajgi@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your progress and settings (scores, shards, the items you own, sound and graphics settings) are saved on your device. The game has no accounts, and it does not send this information to us. Removing the game removes it, apart from any backup your phone makes for you.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game may collect and share:

  • your device's advertising ID, app set ID and, if applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • how you interact with the game and with ads, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • diagnostic information about how the game and the software perform

Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice under Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details. Google tells the game what you bought, so it can unlock the item and restore it on a new phone. See the Google Privacy Policy.

The game connects to Google Play through Unity IAP, a service of Unity Technologies, which collects your approximate location, identifiers for your device and for you as a player, your purchase history, crash logs and performance data, to make purchases work and for analytics. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

Internet check

The game makes a small request to a Google server to check that you are online and to read the current date for daily rewards. It sends nothing about you beyond what any web request carries, such as your IP address.

How data is protected

The services above encrypt the data they collect while it is in transit.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has used the game in a way that concerns you, write to us at the address above.

Changes

We will update this page if the way the game handles information changes, and change the date at the top.
